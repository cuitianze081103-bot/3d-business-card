# Evan Cui — 3D Business Cards

Interactive 3D business cards built with Three.js.

- **Forest Card** (`/forest`) — a little diorama: lake through the card, rolling hills, shell-textured + instanced grass, trees, mushrooms, QR on the back.
- **Ocean Card** (`/ocean`) — a thin card with two "portal" windows (stencil buffer): a Great Barrier Reef on the front, a deep-sea volcano on the back.

Drag to spin · pinch / scroll to zoom · double-tap to flip · hold to open portfolio.
Edit the `CONFIG` block at the top of each `index.html` to change the name, title and links.
